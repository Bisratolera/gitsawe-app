## Full description — English
Gitsawe is a liturgical companion for the Ethiopian Orthodox Tewahedo Church. Open it each day to read the Gitsawe in Ge'ez, Amharic and English, with the Synaxarium (ስንክሳር) for that day.

• Today's Gitsawe with Ethiopian date, previous/next day navigation
• Synaxarium for every day of the year
• Daily, monthly and seasonal Gitsawe, feasts, Mahlet service and teachings
• Search across readings, verses and the Synaxarium
• Daily reminder at the time you choose
• Home-screen widgets
• Audio lessons with background playback
• Bookmarks, share as text or image, dark mode, adjustable text size
• Amharic and English interface
• Works offline. No account, no ads, no tracking.

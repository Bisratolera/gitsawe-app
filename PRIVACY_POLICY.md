# Privacy Policy — Gitsawe

_Last updated: 13 October 2026_

Gitsawe ("the app") does not collect, store, share or transmit any personal information.

**What the app does not do**

- It has no accounts or sign-in.
- It contains no advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or third-party tracking SDKs.
- It does not access your contacts, photos, microphone, camera or location.

**What stays on your device**

Your preferences (language, theme, text size, reminder time) and bookmarks are stored locally on
your device with Android's standard app storage. They are never sent anywhere. Uninstalling the app deletes them.

**Network access**

All readings are bundled inside the app. The internet is used only when you press play on an
audio lesson: the audio file is streamed directly from `eathebook.org`, which, like any web
server, can see your IP address and the file you requested. Gitsawe does not send them anything else.

**Notifications**

If you allow notifications, the app shows a daily reminder with the title of the day's reading.
The reminder is created locally on your device; no server is involved. You can turn it off in
Settings or in your system settings at any time.

**Children**

The app is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone, including children.

**Changes**

If this policy changes, the new version will be published in this repository with an updated date.

**Contact**

Questions or requests: open an issue at <https://github.com/Bisratolera/gitsawe-app/issues>.
